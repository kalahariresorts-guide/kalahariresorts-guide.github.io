Kalahari Resorts Locations Overview

Kalahari Resorts operates multiple large-scale waterpark resort destinations across the United States. Each location offers a similar core experience, including massive indoor waterparks, luxury accommodations, dining, and entertainment. However, each resort also has unique features based on region, size, and nearby attractions.

Choosing the right location is an important part of trip planning. Travelers often consider distance, climate, nearby attractions, and pricing when selecting a Kalahari destination. This guide helps you understand each major location so you can choose the resort that best fits your travel goals.

Wisconsin Dells, Wisconsin

The Wisconsin Dells location is the original Kalahari Resort and remains one of the most popular destinations. It is located in a region known as the “Waterpark Capital of the World,” making it a top choice for families and waterpark enthusiasts.

This resort features one of the largest indoor waterparks in the region, along with outdoor waterpark attractions during warmer months. Guests also enjoy arcades, dining options, shopping, and family entertainment. Wisconsin Dells is a strong choice for Midwest travelers looking for a full waterpark-focused vacation.

Sandusky, Ohio

The Sandusky, Ohio location is another major Kalahari destination and is especially popular because of its proximity to Cedar Point amusement park. This makes it an attractive option for travelers who want to combine waterpark fun with roller coasters and outdoor attractions.

Sandusky offers a large indoor waterpark, multiple dining options, and family-friendly accommodations. Many travelers choose this location for extended stays that include both resort activities and nearby attractions.

Pocono Mountains, Pennsylvania

The Pocono Mountains location is a popular choice for East Coast travelers. This resort combines the Kalahari waterpark experience with scenic mountain surroundings, making it appealing for both families and couples.

Guests at this location often enjoy a balance of waterpark entertainment and outdoor recreation in the surrounding area. This makes the Pocono Mountains resort a flexible option for travelers seeking both relaxation and adventure.

Round Rock, Texas

The Round Rock, Texas location is one of the newer Kalahari Resorts and features modern facilities and large-scale attractions. It includes both indoor and outdoor waterparks, making it especially appealing for warm-weather travel.

Located near Austin, this resort is convenient for travelers in the southern United States. The modern design and expanded amenities make it a strong option for families, corporate groups, and large events.

How to Choose the Best Location

When selecting a Kalahari Resorts location, travelers should consider travel distance, seasonal pricing, nearby attractions, and resort size. Some locations may be better for short weekend trips, while others are ideal for longer vacations.

By reviewing detailed information about each destination, travelers can choose a location that aligns with their budget, travel schedule, and vacation goals.
